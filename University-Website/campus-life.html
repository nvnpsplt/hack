Campus Overview

A 500-Acre Integrated Campus

Global Tech University's sprawling 500-acre campus is a self-contained ecosystem of learning, innovation, and recreation. With lush green quads, modern architecture, and state-of-the-art infrastructure, GTU offers an unparalleled environment for holistic development.

From cutting-edge research labs to serene meditation gardens, from Olympic-standard sports facilities to world-class performing arts centres — every inch of GTU is designed to inspire. Our campus is not just a place to study; it's a place to grow, explore, and thrive.

  • 150+ student clubs and organisations
  • 25,000+ student community from 40+ countries
  • 24/7 library with 5 lakh+ volumes
  • 10 hostels with 8,000+ capacity
  • WiFi-enabled across entire campus

Living on Campus

Your Home Away From Home

GTU's hostels are more than just accommodation — they are vibrant communities where students from diverse backgrounds come together, forge lifelong friendships, and grow as individuals.

We offer separate hostel blocks for men and women, with dedicated facilities for international students. All hostels are fully equipped and connected to the university network.

  • Single, double, and triple sharing options
  • 24/7 high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity
  • Attached bathrooms in premium blocks
  • Common rooms with TV, TT, and carrom
  • Laundry facilities on each floor
  • Round-the-clock security and CCTV
  • Medical room with resident doctor
  • Mess with 24-hour hot water supply
🏠

Men's Hostels (7)

Knowledge Hub

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Central Library

Named in honour of the Missile Man of India, GTU's Central Library is one of the finest academic libraries in the country. Spanning 5 floors and 80,000 sq. ft., it is the intellectual heart of the campus.

With over 5 lakh physical volumes, 50,000+ e-books, access to 200+ international journals, and 24/7 digital access, our library supports research at the highest levels.

  • 5 lakh+ books, journals, and theses
  • 500-seat silent study area
  • Group discussion rooms (50+)
  • Digital resource centre with 200 terminals
  • 24/7 access to e-resources via VPN
  • RFID-enabled book tracking system
  • 3D printing and makerspace lab
  • Rare books and archives section
