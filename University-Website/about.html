About GTU

75 years of excellence, innovation, and nation-building

Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1950

Global Tech University was established in 1950 by visionary educationist Dr. Rameshwar Prasad with a singular mission: to create a world-class institution that harnesses the potential of India's brightest minds.

Starting with just two departments — Engineering and Sciences — GTU has grown into a comprehensive research university offering 120+ programs across 25 departments. Today, we stand among India's top 10 technical universities, consistently ranked by NIRF, QS, and Times Higher Education.

Our journey from a small regional college to an internationally acclaimed research university is a story of relentless pursuit of quality, fuelled by the dedication of our faculty, the brilliance of our students, and the generosity of our alumni.

History

Our Journey Through Time

Seven decades of milestones, achievements, and transformation.

1950

University Founded

Dr. Rameshwar Prasad establishes GTU with Engineering and Science departments, welcoming the first batch of 200 students.

1968

Research Centre Established

GTU Research Centre opens, receiving its first DST grant of ₹50 Lakh for electronics research.

1985

Deemed University Status

UGC grants GTU the coveted Deemed University status, enabling autonomous degree programs across all disciplines.

2000

Golden Jubilee & International Expansion

50th anniversary celebrated with the launch of international exchange programs with MIT, TU Delft, and NUS.

2010

NAAC A++ Accreditation

GTU achieves the highest NAAC grade — A++ — recognising exceptional quality across academics, research, and governance.

2018

Innovation Hub Launched

GTU Innovation Hub inaugurated — a 50,000 sq ft incubation centre that has since birthed 200+ startups with ₹500 Cr in funding.

2025

QS Asia Top 100 Ranking

GTU enters the QS Asia Rankings Top 100, cementing its status as one of Asia's premier technical universities.
