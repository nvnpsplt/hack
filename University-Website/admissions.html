How to Apply

Simple 5-Step Admission Process

Our streamlined process ensures you can apply quickly and start your GTU journey with ease.

1

Check Eligibility

Review program-specific eligibility and entrance exam requirements.

2

Online Application

Fill the online application form at admissions.gtu.edu.in and upload documents.

3

Pay Application Fee

Pay the non-refundable application fee of ₹500 online via UPI / Debit / Credit card.

4

Entrance & Interview

Appear for GATE / JEE / CUET or the GTU entrance test and counselling interview.

5

Accept & Enroll

Accept your admission offer, pay the seat acceptance fee, and join GTU!

Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

🎓

B.Tech Programs

• 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Math
• Minimum 60% aggregate marks
• Valid JEE Mains or CUET score
• Age: 17–25 years as on July 1, 2025

📐

M.Tech Programs

• B.E/B.Tech in relevant discipline
• Minimum 60% aggregate (55% for SC/ST)
• Valid GATE score mandatory
• No age limit for PG programs

🔭

PhD Programs

• M.Tech / M.Sc / M.E in related field
• Minimum 60% aggregate marks
• GATE / NET / fellowship mandatory
• Research proposal submission required

Financial Aid

Scholarships & Financial Aid

GTU believes every deserving student should have access to world-class education regardless of financial background.

🥇

Merit Scholarship

Awarded to the top 10% students in each program based on entrance exam rank and academic performance.

Up to 100% tuition waiver
🤝

Need-Based Aid

For students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. Requires income certificate and counselling interview.

25%–75% tuition waiver
🏅

SC/ST/OBC Scholarship

Aligned with National Scholarship Portal. GTU provides additional top-up to government scholarships for eligible candidates.

As per government norms
🌍

International Student Aid

Special scholarships for students from SAARC, ASEAN, and African nations. Limited seats reserved annually.

Up to 50% tuition waiver
🎖️

Sports Excellence Award

For national/international level athletes representing India in recognised sports. Includes coaching and equipment support.

50% tuition waiver
👩‍🔬

Women in STEM

GTU is committed to gender equity. All female students in STEM programs receive a 10% fee waiver by default.

10% base + merit top-up
