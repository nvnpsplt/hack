FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we receive most often. Can't find what you need? Use the contact form above.

Admission to GTU is done through our online portal at admissions.gtu.edu.in. For undergraduate B.Tech programmes, candidates must appear for JEE Main and qualify GTU's cut-off. For postgraduate M.Tech programmes, GATE score is required. MBA admissions are based on CAT/MAT/XAT scores followed by a group discussion and personal interview. Detailed step-by-step instructions are available on the Admissions page.

GTU offers a wide range of scholarships including the Merit Scholarship (for students with 90%+ in qualifying examinations), the GTU Need-Based Grant (for economically disadvantaged students), the Sports Excellence Award (for national/state-level athletes), and Research Fellowships for PhD scholars. Government scholarships including SC/ST, OBC, and minority scholarships are also processed through GTU. Visit the Scholarships section for detailed eligibility and application information.

GTU strongly prioritises on-campus accommodation for all first-year students. With 10 hostel blocks and capacity for 8,000+ residents, we can accommodate the vast majority of applicants. Hostel allotment is done on a first-come, first-served basis among eligible applicants. International students are given guaranteed accommodation. Students should apply for hostel accommodation as soon as they receive their admission offer letter. Contact the Hostel Office at hostels@gtu.edu.in for details.

Absolutely! GTU welcomes prospective students and their families for guided campus tours. Tours are conducted every Saturday from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM (except public holidays). To book a tour, fill in the contact form above selecting "Campus Tour Request" as the subject, or call our Admissions Office directly at +91 80 1234 5679. Virtual tours are also available on our website for those who cannot visit in person.

GTU has a dedicated International Students Office that provides end-to-end support from visa assistance to accommodation, language support, cultural orientation, and academic integration. We have an English-medium curriculum, and additional language support classes are available. The International Students Network connects new arrivals with senior international students for peer mentoring. Contact international@gtu.edu.in for a comprehensive information pack.

The best starting point is always our main reception at +91 80 1234 5678 or info@gtu.edu.in, and we will route you to the correct department. You can also refer to the Departmental Contact Directory table on this page for direct contacts to specific offices like Admissions, Examinations, Hostels, Placements, Research, and Alumni Relations. For urgent matters, our 24/7 student helpline is available at Ext. 999.

GTU actively seeks industry and academic collaborations for research and development. To initiate a collaboration, email a brief proposal to research@gtu.edu.in outlining the research area, objectives, expected outcomes, and timeline. The Research & Innovation Office will assess the proposal and connect you with the relevant faculty or research group. Collaborative MoUs, sponsored research, and consultancy arrangements can all be formalised through the R&I Office.
